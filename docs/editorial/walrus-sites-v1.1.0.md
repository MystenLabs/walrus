**Mainnet** | September 8, 2025

First quilt support. Behind the `quilts-experimental` feature flag, `site-builder publish-quilts`
bundles multiple resources as quilt patches instead of individual blobs, significantly reducing
publishing cost, and the portal derives `quiltpatchid` values to retrieve quilt-based resources.
