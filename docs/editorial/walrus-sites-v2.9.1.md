**Mainnet** | May 7, 2026

Observability patch: every request is counted in `ws_num_requests_made_counter`, and
`walrus_package` can now be dropped from `sites-config.yaml` (or updated), since it is
auto-resolved from the Walrus staking object. No Move contract changes.
