**Mainnet** | January 26, 2026

`deploy --dry-run` output was refined with Sui gas estimates, resources are grouped by size, and
the missing-site-fields error gained context. Updating a site with expired blobs now works, and
the portal no longer trips serializing unexpected exceptions.
